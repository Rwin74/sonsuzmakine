## Editorial rules

- Use the main commercial page for the broad purchase term. Let supporting articles answer narrower product, process and quote questions.
- Keep each page distinct. Do not clone one template for many nut types, cities or keyword permutations.
- Use product and technical facts that can be checked against the current e-catalogue or a current technical drawing. Do not infer capacity or performance from model numbers.
- Make quote pages useful when price is not public: explain configuration, line scope, installation and acceptance criteria rather than inventing a price range.
- Build first-hand expertise evidence from real sample trials, dated original machine photos, named staff and documented projects as the business can supply them. Do not fabricate case studies, authors, test results or customer outcomes.
- Measure in Search Console by landing page and query, separating machine-related terms from legacy unrelated spam terms. Use clicks, impressions, CTR and average position together; confirm changes over a meaningful period because Search Console data is delayed and noisy.
